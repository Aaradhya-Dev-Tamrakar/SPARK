# SPARK proposal coverage and reference audit

Audit date: 2026-10-14. The comparison covers the proposal master `docs/SPARK_Proposal/ThesisReports/thesis_report.tex`, all six numbered proposal chapters, the proposal's included manual bibliography (`BackPages/references_manual.tex`), figures, appendix, and schedule. The 12-entry `references.bib` is not the bibliography compiled into the proposal and was not used as a substitute for the 20 manual entries. The proposal sources and its compiled PDF were preserved.

## Remaining verification limits

- This audit did not retrieve full primary text for every source. Unmatched records are clearly marked; exact claims attached to them are not used in either report.
- Nho, Wang, and Kumar reference records remain unmatched to primary records; the exact claims attributed to them are omitted.
- The proposal's Gantt chart and its PDF were not changed. The defense reports contain no Gantt chart and no obsolete calendar schedule.
- Existing evidence limitations remain: no physical MCU inference, approved cohort, robot simulation, fresh training, battery test, or clinical validation was performed for these report edits.
